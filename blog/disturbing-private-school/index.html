Disturbing Private School

2026-10-03

I genuinely wanted to make this post because many of our youth have suffered, and that includes me. The system we are in is genuinely "A DISASTER"! This "EDUCATION SYSTEM" (it is genuinely not a system, and calling it one is the biggest crime!) is one where we have seen many people cram for a particular thing just to earn certifications and get a job, and it starts at the school or BOARDS level too.


This isn't about the system we live in, but it hurts me that some of our youth aren't being educated because of the pressure placed on them. I struggled with this myself, as many others did. (I'll clear up any misconception about what I mean shortly.) Some parents take pride in educating their kids with their own money, but others can't properly educate their children at all, because of the pressure that falls on these innocent kids.


I have suffered because of this system, and the same goes for my sister. Today she wasn't allowed to write her exam because my father hadn't paid her fees. My father had an accident while travelling on his scooty, and he told the teachers he would pay the fees. Still, they didn't let her write the exam. Because of this, I'm really eager to share my words through this blog.


(I know nobody will see this blog because I'm not fucking famous, and no one would check it.) But I really wanted to express these words, because it hurts inside that EDUCATION IS JUST FOR MONEY, NOT FOR EDUCATING STUDENTS. I have also faced situations like this, but my father was able to speak up to them, and he was really afraid of losing my education.


I had really suffered for this and i don't like to name these schools because i might get law-suits and it's really bad how we are in this type of shitty System. they are not just educating students, they are making us a product and making more money.
